## Who I am in threads

<!-- 2-3 lines. Who is talking when you comment on an issue: your
experience level stated plainly, what you are doing in this repo, what
readers can expect from you. This is the register your rules protect. -->

When I comment, it is me, the user speaking. I have experience building AI apps using RAG, fine tuning, etc, and have deployed them so I also understand containerization and Docker. I'm looking to make a meaningful contribution, and readers can expect that I make plain, readable takes on technical subjects.

## Rules I write by

<!-- 3-5 rules, drafted from the lecture's slide-12 moment. Each rule

### Rule: Be specific
- Wrong: "The RAG part seems broken"
- Right: "The logic in ingestion.py is broken, specifically ..."

### Rule: Be repsectful
- Wrong: "Whoever wrote this code is stupid"
- Right: "This code needs some refactoring"

### Rule: Write professionaly
- Wrong: "Yo this is how I reproduced ts"
- Right: "Here's the steps I took to reproduce this issue"
